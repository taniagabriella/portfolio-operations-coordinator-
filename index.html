Weekly checkpoints and one action list

The Coca-Cola Company, Nov 2025 to Nov 2026

Situation

Four projects ran at once across The Coca-Cola Company, its bottler CCEP and three vendors. Next steps were easy to lose between meetings.

What I did

I prepared the agenda for each weekly checkpoint, took the notes in the meeting and sent the action items the same day.

I kept one action list in Excel, shared with my director, and booked 6–10 meetings a week for 5–15 people.

Result

Every next step had a person in charge and a deadline, and I followed up until each item was done. My director had one list to check.

Getting a 2,000+ store rollout live after a 3-week delay

The Coca-Cola Company, Nov 2025 to Nov 2026

Situation

A merchandise rollout of 160,000+ units to 2,000+ stores fell almost 3 weeks behind. Product barcodes were not yet registered in the retail accounts' systems, and production ran long because of the volume.

What I did

I held daily calls, checked vendor production, and chased the channel team for a firm go-live date per account. When a date slipped, I escalated to my director. Progress went out by email.

Result

The accounts recovered in batches over 2 to 3 weeks, and every store went live.

It was my first rollout of this size. I now start chasing dates in the first week.

Onboarding 12 bus operators to online ticketing

Redbus Indonesia, Jun 2021 to Nov 2024

Situation

Bus operators sold tickets offline and did not trust online systems. Each one needed documents, a contract, routes and prices, and a trained team before going live.

What I did

I built an onboarding checklist so no step was missed, and wrote the training guide.

I booked calls across time zones with the India-based IT team 3–4 times a week, and stayed close to every operator with regular calls and a monthly visit.

Result

12 operators onboarded, all active at once, each live within 2 to 4 weeks of setup. Most of the business development team adopted the checklist.

Budget requests and a simple spending record

The Coca-Cola Company, Nov 2025 to Nov 2026

Situation

An IDR 11 billion programme, shared with CCEP as the executing team, with three vendors: event and activation production, branding, and merchandise installation.

What I did

I built the budget bottom-up from a wishlist: every item with its account, quantity, months, cost and a priority of yes or no.

I collected quotations, price lists and proposals from the vendors, prepared the budget requests, and tracked each category from allocation to balance.

Result

For every category, one sheet showed what was allocated, what was already used, what was committed and what was left. Requests and spending stayed in step with the plan.
